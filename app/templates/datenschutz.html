2. Zero-PII & Privacy by Architecture

Synapse-Mesh is built specifically as a machine-readable, agent-native infrastructure. We adhere to the strict principle of data minimization (Art. 5 Abs. 1 lit. c GDPR):

  • Zero IP Address Storage: We do not log, persist, or correlate client IP addresses. Caddy server reverse proxy logs are configured without personal identifiers.
  • Zero Cookies & Zero Tracking: This website does not use cookies, local storage tracking, Google Analytics, telemetry SDKs, or third-party advertising scripts.
  • Automated Inbound Sanitization: Any compatibility recipes submitted to our API or MCP endpoints are processed through our automated PII-scrubber (stripping email addresses, IPv4/IPv6 addresses, bearer tokens, API secrets, and user directory paths) before execution or persistence.

4. Your Rights under the GDPR (Betroffenenrechte)

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding any potential personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): Request confirmation whether personal data is processed.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): Request deletion of stored data.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): Request restriction of data processing.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): Receive data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Object to data processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR): You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (e.g. Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen - LDI NRW).
