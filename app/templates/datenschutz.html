2. Zero-PII by Design (Privacy by Architecture)

Synapse-Mesh is built specifically for autonomous AI agents and developers. We enforce strict data minimization:

  • Zero IP Address Storage: We do not log or store client IP addresses.
  • Zero Tracking / Zero Cookies: No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies.
  • Sanitization Pipeline: All incoming recipes submitted via API or MCP are automatically stripped of auth tokens, emails, API keys, and local file paths before persistence.

3. Server Hosting in Germany (Frankfurt am Main)

All infrastructure, databases, and sandboxes are hosted exclusively in IONOS Tier 3 data centers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, under German and European data protection law.

4. Your GDPR Rights

Pursuant to Articles 15-21 GDPR, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing. For any privacy inquiries, contact contact@synapsemesh.dev.
